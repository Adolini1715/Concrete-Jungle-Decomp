# Concrete Jungle controls: what the original does and what the port changes (2026-10-22)

Companion to claude/pcj-player-mod-map.md (where movement, camera and combat live).

## Stick input
- EXGamePad::StorePadState 0x26ed60 (XBGamepad.obj): each stick axis is multiplied by sqrt(2) and clamped to +-32768, then processed by dead zone mode (EXBaseGamePad +0x284 + pad*2, [0] left, [1] right): 0 radial (13107 after scaling, rescaled to 0..1), 1 per axis (each axis zeroed below 13107 after scaling, i.e. 28% raw, rest rescaled), 2 raw. Menus use mode 0; XItemHandler_Player::GetPadData sets mode 1 for both sticks every frame.
- Consequence of mode 1: at full tilt anything within ~16 degrees of a cardinal snaps onto it (~28 degrees at 60% tilt, ~45 at 40%), and diagonals come out slower than straights. This is most of why movement and camera feel stiff; reviews also cite the auto camera, ice-rink inertia, no movement while aiming and no block.
- Port fix (pcj-native 39c265f, src/cj_pad.c cj_stick): host sticks get a radial dead zone (CJ_STICK_DEADZONE, default 0.12; CJ_STICK_CURVE), then are converted to the raw value the game's current mode maps back to that exact output. CJ_STICK_CLASSIC=1 restores the original behaviour. Face buttons register above 30/255; triggers set their digital bit above 127.

## Other complaints from reviews (not yet addressed)
- Auto camera: "possibly the worst auto-camera, ever" (VideoGamer); it rarely settles and fights the player. Lives in XItemHandler_Camera::DoUpdate 0xc6880 (not decompiled yet). Camera defaults: invert X and Y on.
- Movement inertia ("running around an ice-rink"): CalculateMSpeed / SetInertia / InertiaToRunSpeed in Player.obj.
- Cannot move while aiming in first person; no block, so tough fights are dodge-and-hit; too many moves with several inputs each.
- These are design changes. Candidates for opt-in toggles once Camera.obj and the player movement functions are decompiled.
